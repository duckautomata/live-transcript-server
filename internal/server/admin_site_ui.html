What the actions do

Events
Lists the account's notification events on every channel: triggers, each webhook by name and masked id, how often it has posted and its last error. Delete removes one event; the account keeps the rest.
Unlock
Forgets every address's failed sign-in attempts against the username. Locks are per username and address and expire on their own (one minute, doubling, at most an hour, forgotten after an hour of quiet), so this only matters when someone is waiting right now.
Sign out all
Ends every session of the account. The password still works; whoever holds it signs back in.
Disable / Enable
A disabled account cannot sign in, its sessions are refused and its events stop firing, but nothing is deleted. The reason you give is shown to whoever tries to sign in. Enable undoes it.
Delete events
Removes every event the account owns, on every channel. The account itself stays.
Delete account
Removes the account, its sessions, its events and its delivery history. It cannot be undone; the username becomes free again.
Close registration
Refuses new sign-ups until reopened; existing accounts keep working. This is a runtime switch stored in the database; accounts.disableRegistration in the config closes it regardless.
Audit
Every action here is posted to the admin audit webhook (discord.adminWebhookUrl) and written to the server log, with webhooks masked.
